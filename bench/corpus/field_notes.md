# Field notes — shared cross-domain corpus for bench A/B runs (one idea per bullet; verify numbers before relying on them)

## Water

- Slow sand filters grow a biological layer (schmutzdecke) on top of the sand that removes most pathogens when water flows slowly
- SODIS: clear PET bottles left in full sunlight for about six hours inactivate most waterborne pathogens through UV and heat
- Turbid water blocks UV, so SODIS needs water clear enough to read print through the bottle
- Ceramic pot filters with pores near 0.2 micron strain out bacteria; colloidal silver coatings add disinfection
- Activated carbon adsorbs organic compounds, chlorine and bad taste but does not remove most bacteria
- Moringa seed powder acts as a natural coagulant that settles suspended particles out of water

## Heat and energy

- Rocket stoves use an insulated vertical combustion chamber to create a strong draft and near-complete combustion
- Secondary air injected above a fire burns the smoke and gases that would otherwise escape unburned
- Counterflow heat exchangers recover more heat than parallel flow because the temperature gradient stays high along the whole length
- Copper conducts heat roughly twenty times better than stainless steel
- Thermal mass such as water, stone or earth stores heat during the day and releases it slowly at night
- A falling weight releases energy equal to mass times gravity times height; 10 kg falling 2 m is only about 200 joules
- Phosphorescent pigments charge under light and glow for hours without any power source

## Cooling and shelter

- Evaporative cooling through porous clay lowers temperature most when the air is hot and dry
- The zeer pot uses two nested clay pots with wet sand between them to keep food cool without electricity
- Windcatchers draw air into a building and can pass it over water to cool it
- Light-colored and reflective roofs reject solar heat; radiant barriers block heat radiation from hot roofs
- Night-sky radiative cooling lets surfaces facing a clear sky drop below air temperature
- Sandbags fail by seepage between bags; a continuous plastic membrane on the water side stops most leakage

## Mechanics and structures

- A block and tackle multiplies force by the number of rope segments supporting the moving block, minus friction losses
- A capstan or bollard lets a person hold large loads because friction grows exponentially with rope wraps
- Crumple zones protect occupants by extending the stopping distance, which lowers peak deceleration
- Folded paper (origami tubes, honeycomb) becomes far stiffer than flat paper of the same weight
- Triangulated frames resist racking; rectangles need diagonal bracing to stay square
- Hose clamps, bolted plates and lashing join metal tubes without welding
- A bicycle's steering is sensitive to weight on the front wheel; loads carried low and behind the rear axle preserve handling

## Biology, food and health

- Most mold and bacteria need water activity above about 0.9; drying, salting or sugaring food drops it below that
- Lactic acid fermentation lowers pH enough to keep vegetables safe for months without refrigeration
- Seeds stay viable longest when kept dry and cool; sealed jars with a desiccant such as dried rice or silica gel help in humid climates
- Wood ash is strongly alkaline and has been used for food preservation and soap making
- Honey is naturally antimicrobial because of low water activity and hydrogen peroxide release, and has long been used on wounds
- Alcohol hand sanitizer needs at least 60% alcohol to be effective
- Mold that grows along wall bases and behind furniture often signals a hidden moisture source

## Sensing, comms and instruments

- A diffraction grating, such as the surface of a cut CD or DVD, spreads light into a spectrum
- Fluorescent tube light has sharp mercury emission lines at known wavelengths that can calibrate a spectrometer
- Red cabbage juice changes color with pH and works as a cheap indicator
- LoRa radios trade data rate for range and can reach several kilometres line-of-sight on very little power
- Store-and-forward mesh networks deliver messages even when no end-to-end path exists at the moment of sending
- An LED can work as a light sensor because it produces a small current when lit
- Infrared reflectance sensors detect dark lines because black surfaces absorb more infrared

## Materials and waste

- HDPE and PP plastics melt at moderate temperatures and can be pressed into solid shapes; PVC releases toxic chlorine compounds when heated
- Ecobricks pack plastic waste tightly into PET bottles to make building blocks without melting
- Adding sand to melted plastic increases compressive strength and reduces the plastic needed
- Charcoal is a porous form of carbon that can be activated by heating with steam or chemicals
